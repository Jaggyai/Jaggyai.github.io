Before you offer: BC due diligence

  • Court-ordered sale? Offers need court approval, usually with no financing or inspection conditions, and another bidder can show up at the hearing. Ask the listing realtor for the order and the hearing date.
  • Title search (LTSA / myLTSA): check charges, liens, builders' liens, covenants, a Certificate of Pending Litigation, and who actually holds the mortgage.
  • BC Assessment: compare the assessed value, but do not use it as ARV. Use sold comps within 6 months and about 1 km.
  • Condition risks: oil tank, asbestos or knob-and-tube in older homes, drainage, unpermitted work, leaky-condo / envelope history, and strata minutes and depreciation report for strata units.
  • You are a flipper here: do not assign the contract for a fee without checking BC licensing rules (BCFSA) or a BC real estate lawyer. Buying and renovating yourself is the clean route.
  • Taxes beyond this tool: flip profit is generally business income, and the federal residential flipping rule can apply. Talk to an accountant. GST can apply to substantially renovated or new homes.
