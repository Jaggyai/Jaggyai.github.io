IIIThe Program

Let’s Write A New Book of Life

A 6-month, live community program — not a self-paced course. Six chapters, one a month, each one mapped to the real arc of reinvention. Every woman begins with her own intake: what she wants the next five years to look like, and what “unstoppable” means for her.

01

The Collapse

Naming what fell apart — and finally acknowledging it fully.

02

The Reckoning

Confronting the excuses — “no money,” “no time,” “too late” — until belief shifts.

03

The Vision

Getting radically clear on what life looks like five years from now.

04

The Blueprint

Reverse-engineering that vision into concrete, sequential steps.

05

The Leap

Taking the first real action — launching, deciding, starting.

06

Unstoppable

Locking in accountability so momentum outlasts the program.

IVThe Movement

Full Circle

You didn’t just rewrite your book of life. Now you help someone else start theirs. This is how a program becomes a movement — one woman holding another woman’s hand, again and again.

  • 1

    Invite the Next Chapter

    Every graduate is challenged to bring in one woman facing her own crossroads.

  • 2

    Welcomed at Lower Cost

    The woman she invites joins at a reduced rate, or free for a set period.

  • 3

    Become a Guide

    Graduates can step into a mentor role in the community — or, if it’s the right fit, into the business itself.

We come full circle, one woman holding another woman’s hand — knowing we are enough, we are love, we are strong, we are unstoppable.

Now she is bringing every chapter of that story into one place: a memoir, a program, and a movement, built for every woman who is ready to write a new book of life.
