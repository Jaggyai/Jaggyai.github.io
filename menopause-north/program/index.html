Menopause North provides education, not medical care. Carol is not a physician, nurse practitioner or Registered Dietitian. Nothing in this program is a diagnosis, a treatment plan, or a personalised nutrition or exercise prescription — it is a guide to understanding menopause and to working effectively with your own healthcare providers in Canada. Always consult your physician before changing medication, nutrition or physical activity, particularly if you have an existing health condition.
