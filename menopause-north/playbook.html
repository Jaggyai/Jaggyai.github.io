The constraint that shapes everything

The credential problem, and the way through it

Carol has lived experience and no certification yet. That rules out one kind of course and opens up a better one.

Off the table: personalised meal plans, macro prescriptions, individual exercise programming, anything that reads as diagnosis or treatment. In BC, “dietitian” is a protected title regulated by the CHCPBC, and coaching without a credential must never present as clinical care.

On the table, and far more valuable: education, navigation, and advocacy. Nobody in Canada is teaching women how the Canadian system actually works — how to prepare for the appointment, what PharmaCare now covers, what to do when a doctor dismisses them, how to claim it through benefits. That course requires no clinical licence, and no US competitor can make it.

Reframe the product

This is not a menopause fitness course. It is a navigation course for the Canadian menopause system, with a strength and nutrition education module inside it. That framing is honest about Carol’s credentials, legally clean, uncontested in this market, and — because 72% of Canadian women who sought medical advice found it unhelpful — it solves the problem they actually have.

Two rules run through every lesson. First, Carol speaks as a woman who went through this, never as a practitioner: “here’s what I did,” “here’s what I wish I’d known,” “here’s the question I should have asked.” Second, every module ends with the same line, on screen and in the workbook: this is education, not medical advice — take it to your doctor. That line is not a legal fig leaf. It is the product’s actual promise: walk in prepared.

Build order

Ship in six weeks, not six months

  1. Write the Coverage Cheat Sheet first

    It’s research, not filming. It’s the free lead magnet, the SEO engine, and the proof that this brand is Canadian. It also gives Carol something to promote before a single lesson exists.

  2. Film Modules 02 and 03 next

    The appointment script and the coverage map. These are the two modules no competitor has, they need no credential, and they are the ones that will show up in testimonials. If you only ever ship these two, you still have a product.

  3. Record Carol’s story as Module 01

    Film it in one sitting, unscripted, with a written outline. Her credibility is the lived experience — over-produced kills it. Phone on a tripod, window light, one take per lesson.

  4. Build the workbook before Modules 04–07

    Writing the downloads forces the remaining lessons to have a point. A module that doesn’t produce a worksheet is usually a module that doesn’t need to exist.

  5. Film 04, 05, 06, 07 in two days

    Batch it. Same outfit changes, same setup. Module 05 gets the PAR-Q+ gate built into the platform, not just mentioned on screen.

  6. Run a founding cohort at $97

    Fifty seats, closed after a week, in exchange for a testimonial and a completed Symptom Inventory at day 90. That’s $4,850 and — more valuable — fifty pieces of proof and a list of exactly which lessons confused people.

  7. Then Carol starts the certification

    Girls Gone Strong’s Menopause Coaching Specialist runs about USD $1,500, 18 modules, no prerequisites, CanFitPro-recognised CEUs, typically 2–4 months. Start it once the course is earning, not before — and let the course pay for it.

The disclaimer, written properly

Put this on the sales page, in the platform, and at the end of every module

Menopause North provides education, not medical care. Carol is not a physician, nurse practitioner, or Registered Dietitian, and nothing in this course is a diagnosis, treatment plan, or personalised nutrition or exercise prescription. It is a guide to understanding menopause and to working effectively with your own healthcare providers in Canada. Always consult your physician before making changes to medication, nutrition, or physical activity — particularly if you have an existing health condition. Coverage information reflects publicly available provincial program details at the date shown and can change; confirm current coverage with your provincial program or pharmacist.

Get liability insurance before the first sale regardless — it is inexpensive for an education product and non-negotiable once Module 05 exists. And put the date of last review on the Coverage Cheat Sheet in the footer; it’s the difference between a trusted reference and a stale PDF.
